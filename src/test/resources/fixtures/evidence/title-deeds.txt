THIS CONVEYANCE is made the 14th day of June 1962 BETWEEN HAROLD ARTHUR GREY of Mill Race Farm, York, farmer (the Vendor) of the one part and LEONARD GREY of 5 Mill Race, York, schoolmaster (the Purchaser) of the other part.

WHEREAS the Vendor is seised in fee simple in possession free from incumbrances of the property hereinafter described and has agreed to sell the same to the Purchaser for the sum of two thousand four hundred pounds.
NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH that in consideration of the sum of 2,400 paid by the Purchaser to the Vendor (the receipt whereof the Vendor hereby acknowledges) the Vendor as beneficial owner HEREBY CONVEYS unto the Purchaser ALL THAT dwellinghouse and garden known as 5 Mill Race in the City of York more particularly delineated on the plan annexed hereto and thereon edged red TO HOLD the same unto the Purchaser in fee simple.
The Purchaser hereby covenants with the Vendor to observe the restrictive covenants contained in a Conveyance dated 3 March 1931 so far as the same are still subsisting.
IN WITNESS whereof the parties have hereunto set their hands and seals. Signed sealed and delivered by the said Harold Arthur Grey in the presence of J. Whitworth, solicitor's clerk, Coney Street, York.
Abstract of title and epitome: root of title 1931 conveyance; this deed; land unregistered at the date hereof.
