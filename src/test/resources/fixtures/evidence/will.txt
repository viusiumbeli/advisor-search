THIS IS THE LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT of me, Margaret Ellen Fenwick, of 8 Sedgwick Lane, Harrogate HG1 4QP, made on 9 May 2011.

1. I revoke all former wills and testamentary dispositions.
2. I appoint my son Tobias James Fenwick and my daughter Eleanor Ruth Fenwick to be the executors and trustees of this will.
3. I give my jewellery to my daughter Eleanor Ruth Fenwick and the sum of 5,000 to each of my grandchildren living at my death.
4. I give the residue of my estate, after payment of my debts, funeral expenses and inheritance tax, to my children Tobias James Fenwick and Eleanor Ruth Fenwick in equal shares absolutely.
Signed by the testatrix in the presence of two witnesses present at the same time, who then signed in her presence: J. Ashworth, solicitor, and P. Ashworth, secretary, both of Princes Square, Harrogate.
