IN THE COURT OF PROTECTION    Case number 1389 4472 T
MENTAL CAPACITY ACT 2005    In the matter of Arthur Leonard Grey

ORDER made by District Judge Patel on 11 March 2026, on the application of Eleanor Ruth Fenwick
The court is satisfied that Arthur Leonard Grey lacks capacity to make decisions about his property and affairs.
IT IS ORDERED THAT Eleanor Ruth Fenwick of 17 Ropewalk Gardens, York YO1 9QT is appointed as deputy to make decisions on behalf of Arthur Leonard Grey that he is unable to make for himself in relation to his property and affairs, subject to the conditions and restrictions set out in this order.
The deputy must obtain and maintain security in the sum of 150,000 and must send an annual report to the Office of the Public Guardian.
The deputy may not sell or charge the property known as 5 Mill Race, York without further order of the court.
