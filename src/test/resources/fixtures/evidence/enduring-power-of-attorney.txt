ENDURING POWER OF ATTORNEY    Enduring Powers of Attorney Act 1985

Donor: Mrs Margaret Ellen Fenwick, 8 Sedgwick Lane, Harrogate HG1 4QP, born 2 November 1941
Attorneys: Tobias James Fenwick (son) and Eleanor Ruth Fenwick (daughter), appointed to act jointly and severally
I appoint my attorneys to act on my behalf in relation to all my property and affairs, with general authority.
I intend that this power shall continue even if I become mentally incapable.
Signed by the donor on 14 June 2005 and by both attorneys on 21 June 2005, each witnessed.
Registered by the Office of the Public Guardian on 2 December 2025 under reference 7XZQ-2201-9130 following notice to the donor and her relatives, the attorneys having reason to believe the donor is becoming mentally incapable.
