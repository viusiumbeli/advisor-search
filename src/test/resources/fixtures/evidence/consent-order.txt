IN THE FAMILY COURT AT LEEDS    Case number LS26D00412    Matrimonial Causes Act 1973
Between Tobias James Fenwick (applicant) and Charlotte Anne Fenwick (respondent)

FINANCIAL REMEDY ORDER made by consent by District Judge Morrow on 6 February 2026
1. The respondent shall pay to the applicant a lump sum of 95,000 within 28 days of the final order of divorce.
2. There shall be a pension sharing order in favour of the applicant of 35 per cent of the respondent's Albion Teachers' Pension Scheme benefits, in the form annexed (P1).
3. The former matrimonial home at 22 Orchard Close, Knaresborough shall be sold and the net proceeds divided 55 per cent to the applicant and 45 per cent to the respondent.
4. Upon compliance, each party's claims for financial provision against the other and against the other's estate are dismissed.
