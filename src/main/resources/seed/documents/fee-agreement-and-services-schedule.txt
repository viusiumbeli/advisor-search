CLIENT FEE AGREEMENT AND SERVICES SCHEDULE

Between Aldgate Wealth Ltd ("the Firm"), registered in England and Wales under company number 09814472, registered office 4 Copperfield Court, Minories Walk, London EC3V 9GA, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (reference 998874), and Ms Jane Roe ("the Client") of 17 Wexcombe Gardens, London N8 4QT.

Date of agreement: 9 May 2025. Client reference: JR-0417.

1. Initial advice charge

For advice culminating in a recommendation that is implemented, the Firm charges 2 per cent of the sums invested, subject to a minimum of £1,800 and a maximum of £9,500. The charge is agreed in writing before work begins and confirmed in the suitability report. Where advice is not implemented, work is charged at £240 per hour against a written estimate.

2. Ongoing advice charge

The ongoing charge is 0.85 per cent per annum of the value of assets under the Firm's advice, calculated monthly on the platform valuation at the last business day of each month, subject to a minimum of £2,400 and a cap of £12,000 in any twelve-month period.

3. Ongoing service

The ongoing charge covers: a full annual review meeting with a written suitability assessment; portfolio rebalancing twice yearly and after any material market event; tax year end planning each February and March, covering ISA and pension allowances and capital gains management; and ad hoc queries by telephone or email, answered within two working days. Requests falling outside this schedule are quoted separately in advance.

4. Collection of fees

Fees are deducted monthly from cash held within the Client's platform account under facilitated adviser charging. If cash is insufficient, units are sold from the largest liquid holding. The Client may instead settle by invoice within 30 days by writing to accounts@aldgatewealth.example.

5. VAT

Where the Firm acts as an intermediary with a view to arranging investment transactions, its charges are exempt from VAT. Standalone reports or planning work not connected to intermediation carry VAT at the prevailing rate, currently 20 per cent, shown separately on the invoice.

6. Effect of portfolio value changes

Because the ongoing charge is a percentage, the amount payable rises and falls with the portfolio. On a £600,000 portfolio the charge is £5,100 a year, about £425 a month; at £250,000 it is £2,400 under the minimum; above £1,411,765 the cap applies. No balancing charge or refund is made for movements within a month.

7. Termination

The Client may terminate at any time with immediate effect by written notice. The Firm may terminate on 30 days' written notice. Fees accrued to the termination date remain payable; there is no exit penalty and no refund of the initial charge.

8. Complaints and compensation

Complaints should be addressed to the Compliance Officer at the registered office or complaints@aldgatewealth.example. The Firm will acknowledge within five business days and respond finally within eight weeks. If dissatisfied, the Client may refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service without charge. The Firm is covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme; investment business claims are protected up to £85,000 per person.
