Silverbirch Assurance
Policy Schedule - Whole of Life Cover

Policy number: PLC-88213

This schedule, together with the policy conditions booklet (reference SB-WOL-2025-4), forms the legal contract between Silverbirch Assurance and the policyholder. Please keep it in a safe place and read it alongside the trust deed described below.

Life assured: Mrs Amara Okonkwo, date of birth 14 June 1979
Policyholder: the trustees of the Amara Okonkwo Family Trust
Type of cover: whole of life assurance, non-profit, premiums payable throughout life
Sum assured: GBP 450,000, payable on the death of the life assured
Monthly premium: £212.35, collected by Direct Debit on the 5th of each month
Commencement date: 5 September 2025

Trust arrangement

Policy PLC-88213 was written into a discretionary trust from outset, executed on 5 September 2025. The trustees are Mr Chinedu Okonkwo of 8 Alderman's Walk, Reading RG1 7JS, and Ms Fiona Trethowan, solicitor, of Trethowan Legal LLP, Reading. Because the policy is held in trust, the sum assured is paid to the trustees and does not normally form part of the life assured's estate.

Beneficiaries

The discretionary class of beneficiaries comprises the children of the life assured, Ngozi Okonkwo and Emeka Okonkwo, together with any grandchildren born during the trust period. A letter of wishes dated 5 September 2025 is held with the trust deed.

Accidental death benefit rider

An accidental death benefit rider is attached to this policy. If death results directly from an accident and occurs within 90 days of it, an additional GBP 150,000 is payable over and above the sum assured. The rider ceases on the policy anniversary following the life assured's 70th birthday.

Exclusions

No benefit is payable where death results from suicide within the first 12 months of cover, from active participation in war or civil unrest, or from a criminal act committed by the life assured. The accidental death rider additionally excludes death arising from the hazardous pursuits listed in section 9 of the policy conditions, including motorsport and unroped climbing.

Claims

To make a claim, the trustees should contact the claims team at claims@silverbirch-assurance.example, or write to Silverbirch Assurance, Claims Department, Foundry House, 2 Millbrook Yard, Reading RG2 0FX, quoting policy number PLC-88213 and enclosing the original death certificate.

Annual review

The policy is reviewed each year on 5 September. The next annual review date is 5 September 2026, when the trustees will receive a statement confirming that the premium remains sufficient to support the sum assured.
