Adviser Note (internal - not part of the client certification)

Prepared by Priya Raghavan, Chartered Financial Planner (p.raghavan@ashcombewealth.example).

Mr Wisniewski arrived in the United Kingdom on 15 September 2025 to begin full-time employment in London. Split year treatment applied for 2025/26 under Case 5 of the statutory residence test (starting full-time work in the UK), so the UK part of the year runs from 15 September 2025 and Polish-source income arising before that date falls outside the UK charge.

He remains within the Polish system because of the retained Krakow flat, which is let at approximately PLN 3,200 per month. Under Article 4 of the UK/Poland double taxation agreement the tie-breaker resolves residence in favour of the United Kingdom from arrival, as his permanent home and centre of vital interests are now here. Article 6 gives Poland primary taxing rights over the rental income; the same income is reportable on his UK Self Assessment return with credit relief for Polish tax paid. Both TINs above have been verified against his Polish identity card and his HMRC correspondence. Next review of this certification is scheduled for February 2027 or on any notified change, whichever is earlier.
