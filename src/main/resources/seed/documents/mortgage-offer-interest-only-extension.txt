We are pleased to offer you a new mortgage product extending your existing interest-only loan. This offer follows the advised recommendation made by our mortgage adviser, Ingrid Saether, on 4 September 2025.

Property and loan

The mortgaged property is 14 Ullswater Gardens, Harrogate, valued for lending purposes at £745,000 on 2 September 2025. The loan amount is £412,000 on an interest-only basis, a loan-to-value of 55.3 per cent. Your current term is due to end on 31 January 2027; under this offer it is extended by ten years to 31 January 2037. A product fee of £995 is payable on completion and is not added to the loan.

Interest rate and monthly payment

The rate is fixed at 4.29 per cent a year until 31 October 2030. It then reverts to our Standard Variable Rate, currently 7.24 per cent, which can rise and fall for the remainder of the term. Your monthly payment during the fixed period will be £1,472.90. If the loan reverted to today's Standard Variable Rate the payment would be £2,485.73. Because this is an interest-only loan, your payments do not reduce the capital: £412,000 will remain outstanding at the end of the term.

Repayment strategy

You have declared that the capital will be repaid from the sale of your stocks and shares ISA and general investment account held with Meridian Vale Investments, valued at £318,400 on 26 August 2025, with the sale of the property and downsizing as a contingency. Before completion you must provide investment statements dated within 35 days. We will review the continued credibility of your strategy at least once during the term and may request updated statements. For downsizing to remain acceptable as a contingency, our policy requires minimum property equity of £250,000; your current equity of £333,000 satisfies this.

Affordability

We assessed affordability against your verified pension and consultancy income of £96,500 a year and your declared committed expenditure. In line with our responsible lending policy we tested the loan at a stress rate of 8.49 per cent, under which the notional monthly payment would be £2,914.90. The application passed this assessment.

Early repayment charges

If you repay all or part of the loan before 31 October 2030, a charge applies to the amount repaid: 5 per cent before 31 October 2026, then 4, 3, 2 and 1 per cent in each subsequent year of the fixed period. You may overpay up to 10 per cent of the outstanding balance in each calendar year without charge.

Offer expiry

This offer is valid until 17 March 2026. Completion must take place by that date or a new application will be required.

Conditions to be satisfied before completion

First, provision of the repayment strategy evidence described above. Second, buildings insurance with a minimum reinstatement value of £486,000, with the Society's interest noted. Third, redemption of the existing product on this roll number, with any early repayment charge under it waived as an existing-borrower transfer. Fourth, a completed direct debit mandate. Fifth, confirmation from your solicitor, Aldercroft Legal LLP, that title is clear and no second charge is registered.
