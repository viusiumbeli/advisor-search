SUMMARY OF ASSURED SHORTHOLD TENANCY AGREEMENT

Property: 22 Rookery Lane, Leicester LE2 8FD
Prepared by: Granby Gate Lettings, 14 Halford Row, Leicester LE1 6TQ (lettings@granbygate.example)
Prepared for: the landlord's records, 8 October 2025

This summary is for reference only; the signed tenancy agreement dated 22 September 2025 prevails.

Parties

Landlord: Dr Priya Raghunathan, whose address for service is care of Granby Gate Lettings at the address above. Tenants, who are jointly and severally liable: Mr Callum Brice and Ms Adeola Okafor.

Term and break clause

A fixed term of twelve months, from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026. Either party may end the tenancy early under the break clause by giving not less than two months' written notice, expiring on or after 31 March 2026. If neither side serves notice, the tenancy continues after the fixed term as a statutory periodic tenancy running from month to month.

Rent

£1,150 per calendar month, payable in advance on the first day of each month by standing order to the agent's client account. Interest may be charged on rent more than fourteen days overdue, at 3 per cent above the Bank of England base rate, in line with the Tenant Fees Act 2019. The rent is fixed for the term.

Deposit

£1,326, equal to five weeks' rent, was received on 22 September 2025 and protected within the statutory thirty days in the Hollowbrook Deposit Protection scheme, a government-authorised custodial scheme, under reference HDP-88214-RL. The prescribed information was served on both tenants on 26 September 2025 and signed acknowledgements are held on file.

Inventory

An independent check-in inventory and schedule of condition was prepared by Fosse Inventories Ltd on 1 October 2025, running to 41 pages with 214 photographs. Two minor amendments concerning the garden shed were accepted, and the inventory was signed by all parties on 8 October 2025.

Repairing obligations

The landlord is responsible, under section 11 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, for the structure and exterior, and for installations supplying water, gas, electricity, sanitation, space heating and hot water. She must also provide an annual gas safety record (next due September 2026), maintain the electrical installation condition report (valid to March 2030), and keep smoke alarms on each storey and a carbon monoxide alarm by the boiler. The tenants must keep the interior clean and the garden tidy, replace light bulbs and alarm batteries, ventilate the property to prevent condensation, report defects promptly, and make no alterations or redecoration without written consent.

Permitted occupiers and pets

Ms Okafor's nine-year-old daughter is the only named permitted occupier. No other occupiers, lodgers or subletting are allowed, and short-term holiday letting of any kind is prohibited. One neutered cat is permitted on condition that the tenants provide evidence of professional flea treatment and cleaning at the end of the tenancy. No other pets may be kept without the landlord's written consent, which is not to be unreasonably withheld.

Right to rent

Checks were completed on 18 September 2025, before the agreement was signed. Mr Brice's British passport was verified in person; Ms Okafor's status was confirmed through the Home Office online service using a share code, giving a continuous statutory excuse. Copies of both checks are retained by the agent.

Notice provisions

The landlord must give at least two months' notice under section 21 using Form 6A, not expiring before the end of the fixed term unless the break clause is used, or may rely on section 8 grounds where they arise. The tenants may use the break clause during the fixed term and thereafter may give one month's notice ending on the last day of a rental period. All notices must be in writing to the stated addresses for service.

Deposit return

A check-out inspection against the inventory will take place within five working days of the tenancy ending. The agent will propose any deductions within ten working days; deductions are limited to rent arrears, damage beyond fair wear and tear, cleaning to the check-in standard and replacement of lost keys. The agreed balance is released by the scheme within ten days of agreement, and any dispute goes to the scheme's free adjudication service, where the burden of proof rests on the landlord.
