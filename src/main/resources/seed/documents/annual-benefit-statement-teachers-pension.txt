This statement summarises the benefits you have built up in the Scheme. It is not a guarantee of payment; benefits will be calculated under the Scheme regulations in force when they come into payment.

Reckonable service

Your total reckonable service at the statement date is 21 years 212 days. Of this, 12 years 145 days was built up in the final average salary section before that section closed on 31 March 2015, and 9 years 67 days has accrued in the career average section since 1 April 2015.

Final average salary section

Normal pension age in this section is 60. Your final average salary, based on the best consecutive 365 days of pensionable pay in your last ten years, is £48,214. Your accrued annual pension is £7,472 a year, with an automatic tax-free lump sum of £22,416, being three times the pension, payable at normal pension age.

Career average section

Normal pension age in this section is your State Pension age, currently 67. Your accrued annual pension, including the revaluation applied on 1 April 2025, is £6,893 a year. There is no automatic lump sum in this section, but you may exchange pension for cash at retirement at £12 of lump sum for each £1 of pension given up.

Early retirement

Benefits taken before normal pension age are reduced for early payment. Current factors reduce the final average salary pension by approximately 4.1 per cent for each year taken before age 60, and the career average pension by between 4.6 and 5.0 per cent for each year taken before age 67. Factors are reviewed periodically and may change.

Survivor benefits

On your death an adult survivor's pension of £3,571 a year would be payable to a surviving spouse or civil partner, with children's pensions payable in addition where eligible. A death-in-service grant of three times your pensionable salary, currently £144,642, would also apply while you remain in service.

Transfer value

Your cash equivalent transfer value at 8 August 2025 is £198,455. This figure is guaranteed until 14 November 2025; after that date it must be recalculated and may be higher or lower.

Important warning on transfers

Your benefits are safeguarded benefits worth more than £30,000. By law you must take advice from an adviser authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority with pension transfer permissions before transferring to a flexible arrangement. The Scheme cannot process a transfer without written confirmation that this advice has been received.
